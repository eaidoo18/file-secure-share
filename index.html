Secure Share

Turn a PDF into a password-protected PDF with a watermark and a validity date. It opens in any PDF reader and asks for the password first. Your file never leaves your device.

File

Have a Word file? Export it as PDF first, then choose it here.

Password

Send the password by a different channel than the file.

Watermark

Stamped diagonally across every page.

Validity date

Shown on the document, not enforced. A PDF can’t lock itself after a date.

Encryption: AES-256 PDF standard. Works offline once loaded.
